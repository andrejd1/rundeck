Questions

Which watches does it work on?

Amazfit watches on Zepp OS 3.6 or newer that support workout extensions (data pages from apps inside the native workout). It's developed and tested on the Cheetah 2 Ultra.

Does it replace the Amazfit running workout?

No. You run the normal Amazfit workout; RunDeck is one of its data pages. Recording, GPS, laps, sync to Zepp and Strava all stay native.

Where do my heart-rate zones come from?

By default from the zones set on your watch (Zepp OS 4.2 and newer). Older watches estimate them from your age in the Zepp profile. You can also set max HR, threshold HR or your own zones.

Can I show GAP, vertical oscillation or ground contact time?

Not yet — Zepp OS doesn't share those with apps. Everything RunDeck shows comes from the watch itself, never estimated.

What happens after the 5 free runs?

The screen keeps heart rate, your main pace and time, and a note to unlock. Everything comes back as soon as you enter a key — even mid-run.

Is my data sent anywhere?

Your workout data never leaves the watch. The only thing sent online is your license key, to Polar, when you activate it.
