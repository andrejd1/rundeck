# RunDeck privacy

- **Workout data** (heart rate, pace, distance, altitude, power) is read on the watch to draw the
  screen and never leaves it. RunDeck keeps only the screen settings, the trial counter and the
  unlock flag on the watch.
- **Settings** (thresholds, zones, target) stay in the Zepp app's settings storage on your phone.
- **License key:** when you enter a key, the phone sends it to Polar.sh (our reseller and merchant
  of record) together with a label derived from your watch's device id, to activate it — and
  re-checks it at most weekly. Nothing else is sent. Polar's own privacy policy covers the
  purchase itself.
